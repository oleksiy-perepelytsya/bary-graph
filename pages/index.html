The Idea in One Minute

In standard vector indices, relationships often remain implicit in text[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). BaryGraph makes the relation itself a first-class vector object, called a BaryEdge[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

1

BaryEdge Creation

Two concepts form the children of a BaryEdge[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). The edge receives a normalized composition vector of its children and context[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

2

MetaBary Triads

BaryEdges become children of higher-order relational triads (MetaBary)[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span), recursing upward into abstract relational forests[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

3

Efficient Vector Algebra

Higher-level triads use vector algebra on stored vectors[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). No new embedding calls required for recursive steps[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).

Leaf-Level Vector Construction[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
$$\mathbf{v}_{\text{edge}} = \operatorname{normalize}\left(q\mathbf{v}_1 + q\mathbf{v}_2 + (1-q)\mathbf{v}_{\text{context}}\right)$$[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)

Why This Is Different

BaryGraph makes relations, and relations between relations, addressable in the same semantic space as the concepts themselves[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

What It Can Be Used For

BaryGraph operates as an associative atlas or "de-cliche device" for humans and AI agents alike[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span).

1. Cross-Domain Research

Identify analogies worth checking, shared mechanisms worth formalizing, or unexpected vocabulary across disciplines[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).

Example query: Coral reefs, legal precedent, & error-correcting codes $\rightarrow$ resilience through redundancy[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span).

2. Lateral Ideation

Provides deliberate resistance to cliched continuations in creative writing, strategy, and design work[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).

Example query: Treating uncertainty as material for designing resilient rituals or shared decisions[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span).

3. Agentic Semantic Resistance

Acts as an external coordinate system for LLMs, preventing overly smooth or conventional first-pass generations[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).

Gives agents relations they did not generate themselves to justify, operationalize, or test[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).

4. Relational Terrain Exploration

Corpus-agnostic architecture expandable to scientific literature, software repos, legal precedent, or biological ontologies[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span).

Current PoC utilizes English Kaikki/Wiktionary ground-truth relations[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span).
